Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly stores the financial data you voluntarily enter, including expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect any personal information beyond what you provide directly.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the expense tracking features of Spendly — organizing your expenses by category, generating summaries, and displaying your financial information. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your expense data is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for your own data backup. We recommend exporting your data periodically if you need to preserve it.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not integrate with any third-party analytics or advertising services. The only external connections are for font loading from Google Fonts, which does not involve any personal data transfer.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Spendly app or our website.
